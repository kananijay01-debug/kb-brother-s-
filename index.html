Our Story

Preserving Freshness, One Batch At A Time

KB Brother's is a dedicated manufacturer and supplier of dehydrated vegetables, fruit powders, spice powders and food ingredients, built on a simple promise: preserve nature's freshness without compromising on purity. Based in Dhari, Gujarat — a region known for its fertile agricultural belt — we work closely with local farmers to source raw vegetables, fruits and spices at peak ripeness, then dehydrate and mill them into finely processed powders that retain their natural colour, aroma and nutritional value.

Every batch that leaves our facility passes through a disciplined process of cleaning, sorting, controlled dehydration, grinding and quality testing, carried out in a hygienic, moisture-controlled environment. We supply food manufacturers, spice blenders, exporters and bulk buyers who need consistent quality, reliable volumes and on-time delivery.

Our name stands for a simple idea — dehydration, and it's powder — turning fresh produce into a stable, concentrated ingredient that food businesses can trust, batch after batch.

🎯
Our Mission
To be a dependable ingredient partner for food businesses across India and abroad, delivering dehydrated vegetable, fruit and spice powders that are consistently pure, hygienic and true to their natural character.
🔭
Our Vision
To build KB Brother's into a recognised name in dehydrated food ingredients — known for disciplined quality control, ethical sourcing and long-term partnerships with our clients.
